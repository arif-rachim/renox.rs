2. What this notice covers

This notice covers the site renoxium.com, the dashboard at dashboard.renoxium.com, the API at api.renoxium.com and the Renoxium connector at mcp.renoxium.com. It does not cover the apps our customers run on Renoxium: the customer who runs an app decides what it collects and is the controller of that data, and we process it on their behalf under the data processing agreement.

3. What we collect and why

WhatThe dataWhy

To sign you in, keep your account secure, and tell you when a new browser signs in.

To let the assistant act for you within what you approved, and to keep a record of what it did.

4. What an AI assistant connected through the connector receives

You choose what an assistant may do on the consent page, and it can never do more than your own role in the workspace allows. It sees only the tools your approval allows and receives only what those tools return. A tool that changes something first returns a plan in words and runs only when it is called again with that plan's confirmation. An assistant never receives a secret value: it can list your secrets' names, and it is never allowed to write secrets or create tokens.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

The service runs on one server. Every request reaches it through Cloudflare, which terminates the encrypted connection. Backups are kept on the same server's disk. The providers that process data for us are listed as the data processing agreement states in its section 4.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account: no fixed period yet. A session expires 30 days after sign-in, or after 24 hours without use.
  • Waitlist: no fixed period yet. The emailed confirmation link works once and for 7 days.
  • Usage and logs: the record of changes is append-only and is kept when a workspace is erased; it holds ids, never your data. The hourly counts are kept for billing, also after an app is deleted.
  • Billing: nothing is held.
  • Connector: an access token lasts one hour; the refresh token lasts 30 days from its last use, and you can revoke the grant at any time from the dashboard's token list. The record of tool calls is kept like the record of changes.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by writing to support@renoxium.com. You can also act yourself: a workspace's databases can be exported at any time, and the workspace owner can erase the workspace, which deletes its databases, files, replicas and backups within 24 hours while the servers are reachable. Members of an erased workspace keep their own accounts.

8. Cookies

Every page of the site and the dashboard sets two cookies the platform needs to work, both ending when you close the browser: __Host-renox_sid, a random id that holds nothing about you, and __Host-renox_csrf, the token that protects forms against requests from other sites. The dashboard sets three more: __Host-renox_session keeps you signed in, __Host-renox_device recognises a browser you signed in from before (for the new sign-in email), and renox_lang keeps the language you chose. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.
