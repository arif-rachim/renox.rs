Modules instead of rewrites

Sign people in with passwords and passkeys.

renox add auth brings the login and account pages, passkeys and magic links, with no auth code of your own.

Put people in organisations with roles.

renox add org brings invites by email, a role per member and an organisation switcher.

renox add billing brings plans, subscriptions, checkouts and refunds, with Stripe UAE first.

Run the back office in one place.

renox add admin brings the users page, roles and the audit log behind one admin area.
