The scripts need Node 22+ and a Chrome or Chromium. They write their working
files to .taste-skill/ in the project root (ignored by git). In a cloud session
running as root, Chromium needs --no-sandbox, which inspect.mjs does not pass;
point CHROME_PATH at a wrapper:

  printf '#!/bin/sh\nexec /opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome --no-sandbox "$@"\n' > ~/.taste-skill/chrome
  chmod +x ~/.taste-skill/chrome
  CHROME_PATH=~/.taste-skill/chrome node .claude/skills/design-taste-frontend/scripts/inspect.mjs <url>

Its house rules (no monospace labels, no hairlines, no pill badges, no
eyebrows, no shadows at rest) conflict with parts of renox-ui v2 (ADR D27).
ADR D27 binds Renoxium's own pages; apply the skill's rules there only where
they do not contradict it.
