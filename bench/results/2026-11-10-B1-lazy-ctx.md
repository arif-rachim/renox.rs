**Task 2 was not done.** Its condition is "more than 0.05 MB per module", and a facade takes 0.0034 MB, fifteen times less. That figure did not move with #633 or #640, because neither changed `moduleFields`. As on 2026-11-09, no facade is built at isolate creation. `newRequestState(..).ctxOf(m)` builds a module's frozen ctx the first time a request reaches that module, and caches it for that request. A request that touches 12 modules builds 0.041 MB of facades, which become garbage when the request ends. B1's `GET /api/products` builds two: `app` and `auth`, through the principal lookup.

What #642's source named as the per-module V8 heap of #344 (≈ 50 KB per module) was the evaluation of the modules' own code. That is #640, which took `shop`'s V8 heap from 4.53 MB to 3.93 MB per isolate (`bench/results/2026-11-10-B1-lazy-module-entries.md`).

## Related

The facades are garbage of every run, not memory an isolate keeps. Their share of B14's per-connection isolate garbage is #680's subject. #680 makes the realtime ctx build its `Request` and `URL` only when read, and has V8 return the heap after a burst of runs.
