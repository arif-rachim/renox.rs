# Unit cost per request — 2026-10-28

The first cost-per-request numbers from real meter records, for `docs/01-business.md` ("Unit economics") and the F7 pricing decision (`docs/15-roadmap.md`, "Pricing model"). Produced by `control/billing/src/unit-cost.ts` (#56).

Hardware: dev container (Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM), git 71fdd2e plus batch S, `renox-worker` release build, Bun 1.4.4 load generator on the same machine. Not `node-small`: relative numbers, not a benchmark result.
Method: `bench/lib/worker.sh setup/start` with one app per worker, 1,000 warm-up requests, then 10,000 measured requests from a closed-loop Bun client at concurrency 1 and 16. Meter records from the file sink (`<data_dir>/meters/`); process CPU from `/proc/<pid>/stat` (user + system) of the worker across the measured requests.
Shop load: 40 % `GET /api/products`, 30 % `GET /api/products/:id`, 20 % `GET /api/products?q=dates`, 10 % `POST /api/cart`, on the 100 seeded products.
Cost inputs (the hypotheses of `docs/01-business.md`): one node at $125/month, 4 vCPU, 60 % of its CPU sellable, egress at $0.09/GB.

## Findings

1. **`cpu_ms` over-counts under concurrency.** At concurrency 16 the metered CPU per request is larger than the whole worker process's CPU per request (hello 0.347 against 0.126 ms; shop 3.758 against 0.689 ms), which is impossible if attribution were exact. With one request in flight it is plausible (isolate CPU below process CPU). This is open item 2 of `docs/contracts/meter.md` ("CPU attribution under concurrency") and the subject of #155 (CPU accounting). Until it is fixed, billing CPU-ms from these records would overcharge busy tenants 3–10×, and the free-tier CPU quota signal (`src/quota.ts`) fires early for them. Reported on #56; no change here.
2. **Egress, not CPU, dominates the cost of a page-sized response.** For `shop` (9 KB JSON responses) egress is $0.81 of the $0.82 per million requests. At these inputs CPU costs about $0.002 (hello) to $0.007 (shop) per million requests from the metered CPU, and $0.0025 to $0.014 from the process CPU at concurrency 16.
3. **Fully loaded CPU per request** (gateway, runtime, SQLite threads; the process column at concurrency 16) is 0.13 ms for `hello` and 0.69 ms for `shop`. At 60 % of 4 vCPU that is about 19 k and 3.5 k req/s per node respectively, or 49 G and 9 G requests per node-month: the CPU per request is not what limits a node's tenant count (`docs/14-benchmarks.md` B2/B3 density is).

These numbers come from one dev machine with the load generator sharing its CPUs. Re-run on `node-small` (#158) before they go into `docs/01-business.md`.
