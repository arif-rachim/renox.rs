# renoxium.com: the copy deck (full replacement, 2026-11-09; issue #675)

Every word below is the page. Nothing of the current page survives except the three measured numbers and the plan table's source (`pricing.toml`).

**Owner decisions (2026-11-09):**

1. **The reader is a business owner, not a technician.** Sections 1 to 6 and 8 to 10 are written for a layperson: every section opens with the *why* (what they live with today, and what changes), and no technical term appears in them. The forbidden list, which the tests check: isolate, V8, SQLite, CNAME, DNS, Apache, MCP, CLI, repository, deploy, htmx, TypeScript, SQL, API, Docker, build step, open source as a bare phrase without its plain meaning. Those words live in section 7 ("Build on Renoxium") and nowhere else.
2. The hero speaks to the owner who already uses ChatGPT or Claude; developers and agencies are the second reader and get section 7.
3. The primary action is **Connect to your ChatGPT or Claude**. The connector is not shipped yet: the button says "coming soon, join the list", the page shows the full experience as the product, and what is live today versus coming is stated in one place (section 3).
4. The conversation shown covers all four demands: the app from a description of the business; the owner's own web address; business email alongside (through a partner, marked as coming); changes on request.
5. Honesty: no testimonials, logos or invented numbers; the measured numbers carry a link to how they were measured; "if we disappear tomorrow, your app keeps running" is printed only when #668 ships.

The order is the pitch of the positioning canvas (`docs/plans/2026-11-09-positioning-home-services.md`, sections 8, 9 and 14): why, the experience, what is live, the alternatives, what you get, proof, developers, when not to use, price, the ask.

## 5. What you get

Heading: **Finished on day one.**
Why it matters: an app that starts empty is a project; an app that starts finished is a tool. CoolAir in the conversation is FixIt, our complete home-services app: customers and their sites, quotations approved on WhatsApp, jobs and a live day board, the technician's visit on a phone with photos and the customer's signature and a PDF report, tax invoices the FTA accepts with cash, transfer and cheque payments, maintenance contracts that renew themselves, reports, and a back office with your name and colours. English and Arabic. Home services is the first trade; the next ones follow the first customers.
Pictures: four real screens: the quotation approval on a phone, the day board, the technician's visit page, the Arabic tax invoice.

### 5B. Yours, in a way you can check
Heading: **Yours. Not a promise, a fact you can test.**
Why it matters: everyone says "your data is yours"; few let you walk out with it. Your company's data is one file, and you can ask for a copy of it any time, today. The software itself is open: anyone can read it, nothing is hidden, and it can be run on a server that is not ours. A complete take-away of your app, its files and its settings, with a guide to run it on your own server, is being built in the open (link: #668). Until that ships we do not print the sentence we want to print; when it ships, it will read: if we disappear tomorrow, your app keeps running.

### 5C. The parts that must never break
Heading: **What your AI is not allowed to touch.**
Why it matters: when an AI improvises your login or your invoices, it works until the day it does not. Sign-in and the second code on the phone, who may see what, payments, tax invoices and VAT, WhatsApp, the record of who did what, backups: these are finished parts that Renoxium keeps correct and up to date. Your app runs in its own sealed room on our servers, so another company's mistake or traffic cannot reach yours. When the UAE e-invoicing rules reach small businesses in 2027, that update is our work, not your project.

### 5D. Made for here
Heading: **Built for a business in the UAE.**
Dirhams and fils; 5 % VAT and your TRN on an invoice numbered the way the tax office expects; credit notes; +971 numbers and TRNs checked as you type; Emirates and areas instead of postcodes; Hijri dates where you want them; Arabic PDFs; WhatsApp for approvals, reminders and sign-in codes.

## 7. Build on Renoxium (developers and agencies; the one technical section)
Heading: **For the people who build apps for others.**
The finished parts in the conversation are modules you install: `renox add auth org billing admin`. Each app runs in its own V8 isolate with its own SQLite file, deploys with one command, and ships `AGENTS.md` and a local MCP server so your coding agent works on it as a colleague who has read the manual. An agency gets client workspaces and white label. TypeScript, HTML and SQL; no build step, no Docker; the whole platform is Apache-2.0.
Code block: `bun create renox fixit --template home-services` · `renox dev` · `renox deploy`.
Links: Getting started · The modules · The reference app · Pricing for agencies

## 8. When not to use Renoxium
Heading: **When not to use Renoxium, and what to pick instead.**
- A large company with its own developers who already build on a big web framework: keep them on it.
- A business that writes thousands of records a second (a trading desk, a sensor network): you need a heavy database from day one.
- A highly interactive app like a design tool or a game: Renoxium is built for business apps, pages and forms.
- Health records: we do not host them.
